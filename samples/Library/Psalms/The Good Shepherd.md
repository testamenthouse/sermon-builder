::: intro
Every one of us has felt lost — and sheep do too. Jesus called himself the good shepherd in John 10:11, and David knew why.
:::

::: scripture Psalm 23:1-3
1 The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.
2 He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters.
3 He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake.
:::

::: point The Lord is my shepherd {slide}
Ownership comes before provision. He is *my* shepherd before he is my supply.
:::

::: illustration The lost lamb
A farmer counted ninety-nine and went out into the dark for one.
:::

::: application
What are you trying to provide for yourself this week that he has promised to provide?
:::

::: transition
If he owns us, he leads us.
:::

::: point He leads me
Green pastures and still waters are not the destination; the shepherd is.
:::

::: point Beside still waters
Rest is something he does *to* us.
:::

::: quote C. H. Spurgeon
The Lord is my shepherd — what a word is that!
:::

::: note {hidden}
Slow down here. Let the room breathe.
:::

::: conclusion
Goodness and mercy do not just wait at the end — they follow.
:::

::: invitation
If you have never let him be your shepherd, today is the day.
:::
